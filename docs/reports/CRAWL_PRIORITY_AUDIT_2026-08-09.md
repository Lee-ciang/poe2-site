## Executive Summary

POE2 Forge has an indexing-priority problem, not a page-count problem. The most important finding is that `/sitemap.xml` is currently served from `public/sitemap.xml`, not from the App Router sitemap generated by `src/app/sitemap.ts`. The served public sitemap contains 51 URLs, all under `/guides/skills/*`, and 5 of those URLs return local 404s. It does not include the homepage, build hub, boss hub, skill hub, guide hub, upgraded boss pages, upgraded skill authority pages, or typed build/skill pages.

This is the clearest likely reason Google continues to index only `/` and `/bosses/executioner` despite many generated static pages. Google is being pointed at a stale, low-priority sitemap dominated by programmatic/draft-style URLs and dead guide routes, while the strongest current authority pages are not present in the served sitemap.

## Top Crawl / Indexing Risks

1. **Stale public sitemap overrides the App Router sitemap.**
   `/sitemap.xml` serves `public/sitemap.xml`, which contains 51 low-priority URLs and excludes the strongest authority pages.

2. **Served sitemap contains 5 local 404s.**
   Google may treat the sitemap as low quality because it asks crawlers to visit nonexistent guide URLs.

3. **Served sitemap is dominated by programmatic draft-style pages.**
   The 46 valid served sitemap URLs are programmatic pages with weak depth, 1 FAQ, and visible low-trust programmatic/draft wording.

4. **Core authority pages are missing from the served sitemap.**
   The upgraded skill and boss authority pages are exactly what should be pushed first, but they are not present in the served sitemap.

5. **Metadata and duplicate-intent signals remain.**
   Duplicate skill slugs, duplicate guide titles, generic SEO descriptions, and `*-ai` guide variants weaken trust and intent clarity.

## Decision Rules

### 1. Keep Observing

Keep observing only if all of the following are true:

- The served sitemap is repaired first.
- GSC confirms the sitemap was read after repair.
- Indexed pages begin moving above 2.
- Non-brand impressions begin appearing for upgraded skill or boss pages.

Observation window after sitemap repair: 7-14 days.

### 2. Reduce Sitemap To A Core Indexable Set

Reduce the sitemap to a core indexable set if:

- GSC continues to show 58+ discovered currently not indexed after the next sitemap read.
- Indexed pages remain at 2.
- Google still recognizes mostly brand and Executioner queries.
- Programmatic pages remain draft-like or visibly programmatic.

Suggested core set: homepage, four hub routes, top 14 upgraded skill guides, six upgraded boss pages, and selected build pages only after their depth improves.

### 3. Strengthen Homepage / Indexed-Page Links

Strengthen homepage and indexed-page links if:

- Sitemap repair is complete but Google still crawls mostly `/` and `/bosses/executioner`.
- Boss pages begin getting impressions but skill guides do not.
- Homepage receives impressions but authority does not flow into `/guides/skills/*`.

Focus link improvements on top 20 priority pages, not programmatic or duplicate-intent pages.

### 4. Downgrade POE2 Forge To Low-Maintenance

Move to low-maintenance mode if, after sitemap repair and 30 days of observation:

- Indexed pages remain at 2-3.
- Non-brand impressions remain minimal.
- Discovered currently not indexed does not improve.
- No authority cluster beyond Executioner shows visibility.

Low-maintenance mode should mean no new content expansion, monthly technical checks, and only targeted updates to pages that receive impressions.
