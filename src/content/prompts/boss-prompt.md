# Boss Guide Prompt

Create a practical Path of Exile 2 boss guide for:

- Boss name:
- Location:
- Difficulty:
- Damage types:
- Known weaknesses:
- Patch or source context:

Follow this structure:

1. SEO title under 60 characters.
2. Meta description under 155 characters.
3. Short summary of what makes the fight dangerous.
4. Overview with location, difficulty, damage types, and weaknesses.
5. Phase breakdown with what changes in each phase.
6. Key mechanics with clear dodge, punish, and recovery advice.
7. Recommended builds with reasons.
8. Rewards, only if verified.
9. Tips for common player mistakes.
10. FAQ with 4-6 search-focused questions.
11. Internal linking ideas to builds and relevant skills.

Rules:

- Prioritize player survival and decision-making.
- Do not invent exact rewards, drop rates, or phase names.
- Mark uncertain mechanics as unverified.
- Explain what to do, not only what the boss does.
- Keep headings clear and SEO-friendly.
