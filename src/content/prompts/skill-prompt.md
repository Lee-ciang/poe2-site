# Skill Page Prompt

Create a practical Path of Exile 2 skill guide for:

- Skill name:
- Category:
- Damage type:
- Weapon requirement:
- Patch or source context:

Follow this structure:

1. SEO title under 60 characters.
2. Meta description under 155 characters.
3. One-paragraph skill summary.
4. Overview with category, damage type, and weapon requirement.
5. How the skill plays in real combat.
6. Scaling stats with uncertainty notes for unverified formulas.
7. Best supports with reasons.
8. Recommended builds and why the skill fits them.
9. Strengths.
10. Weaknesses.
11. Leveling notes.
12. Endgame use.
13. FAQ with 4-6 search-focused questions.
14. Internal linking ideas to related builds, bosses, and skills.

Rules:

- Be specific about the skill's role.
- Avoid fake exact numbers or unverified interactions.
- Separate leveling advice from endgame advice.
- Include practical support and gear logic.
- Use clear uncertainty notes when data has not been tested.
