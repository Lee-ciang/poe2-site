## Overview

Arc is a lightning-based spell that excels at clearing packs quickly thanks to its automatic chaining bolts. It feels powerful early on because it selects targets for you, making it nearly effortless to clear groups without precise aiming. However, its boss damage can plateau unless you invest heavily in scaling stats or specialized gear. Players often find themselves relying on frequent weapon upgrades during leveling to prevent frustrating damage stagnation.

## Scaling Stats

Arc benefits most from increases to Lightning Damage, overall Spell Damage, Cast Speed, Critical Strike Chance, and the effectiveness of Shock applied to enemies.

## Best Supports

- **Lightning Penetration:** Improves damage against lightning-resistant foes, which is critical for tougher enemies and boss encounters.
- **Faster Casting:** Speeds up casts to chain more lightning bolts rapidly, enhancing clear speed and Shock uptime.
- **Controlled Destruction:** Amplifies overall damage at the cost of reduced critical strike chance, offering a straightforward boost. The tradeoff’s exact impact on Arc’s crit synergy needs verification.
- **Added Lightning Damage:** Boosts base lightning damage to increase overall spell hit power and Shock effectiveness.

These supports build around maximizing damage output while maintaining reliable clear and consistent Shock application.

## Recommended Builds

- **Lightning Sorceress:** Focuses on maximizing lightning and spell damage alongside cast speed and crit to push clear speed and damage.
- **Shock Caster:** Prioritizes applying Shock frequently and increasing its effect, often relying on faster casting and added lightning damage to maintain uptime.

## Leveling Use

Arc stands out as a very strong leveling skill due to its automatic chaining, which drastically reduces the hassle of clearing packs and allows for fast progression. One common rookie mistake is under-upgrading caster weapons, which leads to noticeable damage plateaus that slow campaign pacing. Frequent wand or staff upgrades help maintain a smooth damage curve and prevent these frustrating breaks. 

## Endgame Use

Arc remains a popular fast-mapping skill because its chaining mechanic efficiently clears dense encounters without constant target switching. However, it typically struggles on pinnacle bosses where single-target damage and burst are paramount. Experienced players often combine Arc for speed and clear with dedicated single-target setups to tackle tough bosses, addressing its inherent limitations in sustained boss damage.

## FAQ

**Q: Why does my Arc damage feel weak on bosses?**  
A: Arc’s damage often falls off on single-target encounters without heavy investment in critical strike and lightning penetration. Many players switch to separate single-target skills or tweak supports for bosses.

**Q: How important is mana sustain at high cast speeds?**  
A: Mana can become a problem when casting Arc very rapidly, as sustaining high cast speed significantly increases mana costs. Players often manage this via mana regen gear or passive nodes.

**Q: Can I use Arc without a wand or staff?**  
A: No, Arc requires either a wand or staff to cast.

**Q: Are there any must-have supports for Arc?**  
A: Lightning Penetration and Faster Casting are generally the most impactful to boost both damage and clear rate reliably.
