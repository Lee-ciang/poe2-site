# Content Quality Checklist

Use this before adding or updating any build, boss, or skill page.

## Search Intent

- The page answers the likely player question in the first screen.
- The title clearly matches the page type and entity.
- The intro tells players whether the page is relevant to them.

## Original Insight

- The page explains why recommendations matter.
- The content includes tradeoffs, not just advantages.
- The advice prevents common mistakes or wasted upgrades.

## Practical Usefulness

- Builds include gear, skill, leveling, and endgame priorities.
- Boss guides explain what to dodge, when to punish, and how to prepare.
- Skill pages explain scaling, supports, and best-fit builds.

## Avoiding Hallucinated Game Facts

- No invented damage numbers, drop rates, or hidden mechanics.
- Unverified details are labeled as uncertain.
- Rewards and patch-specific changes are verified before publishing.

## Patch Relevance

- Patch-sensitive advice is reviewed after updates.
- Nerfs, buffs, mechanical changes, and item changes are reflected.
- Old recommendations are removed when they no longer apply.

## Internal Links

- Links help the player continue the same task.
- Build pages link to core skills and relevant boss guides.
- Boss pages link to recommended builds and useful skills.
- Skill pages link to builds that actually use the skill.

## FAQ Quality

- FAQ questions reflect real player concerns.
- Answers are concise and useful.
- FAQs do not duplicate the same information from the intro.
- Uncertainty is clearly stated where needed.

## Title And Description Quality

- SEO title is specific and under 60 characters when possible.
- Meta description is specific and under 155 characters when possible.
- Both include the entity name and page type.
- Neither uses clickbait or unverifiable claims.
