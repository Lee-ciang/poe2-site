## Overview

Count Geonor is an early boss check built around melee pressure, cold damage, and arena control. The fight rewards patient dodging and punishes players who attack during long windups.

## Strategy / Usage

Stay at mid-range so lunges and delayed swings are easier to read. Prioritize cold resistance, keep enough recovery available, and attack after completed combos rather than during active weapon animations.

## Strengths

- Clear telegraphs help players learn the fight over repeated attempts.
- Ranged and mobile builds can maintain safer uptime.
- Cold resistance meaningfully reduces incoming pressure.

## Weaknesses

- Panic dodging can put players into follow-up attacks.
- Low-mobility builds may struggle during arena pressure.
- Under-geared characters can run out of recovery before the final phase.

## FAQ

### What damage types should I prepare for?

Prepare for physical and cold pressure. Cold resistance is especially helpful while learning the fight.

### Which builds are good for Count Geonor?

Mobile ranged builds and durable melee builds both work well. The best choice is one that can deal damage without overcommitting during delayed attacks.
