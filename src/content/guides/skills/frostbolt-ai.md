## Overview

Frostbolt is a cold-based projectile skill that excels at single-target damage with moderate clear speed. It launches a shard of ice that pierces enemies, making it effective against linear enemy formations and bosses. Unlike some cold skills, Frostbolt tends to favor precision and critical strikes over raw area damage. Players should focus on boosting cold damage, projectile speed, and critical chance to maximize its potential. While reliable in early mapping, it demands careful investment in supports and gear to remain viable in the endgame.

## Skill Category

Frostbolt falls under projectile spells with cold damage. It is classified as a ranged caster skill that channels the power of cold to impale enemies from a distance. This categorization means it interacts well with projectile damage nodes, cold spell damage clusters, and critical strike bonuses common on caster builds.

## Damage Type

Frostbolt deals primarily cold damage. It has no innate conversion but benefits heavily from increased cold damage, elemental damage, and critical modifiers. The skill benefits from added cold damage supports and effects that enhance chill and freeze to improve crowd control alongside damage output.

## Weapon Requirement

Frostbolt can be cast with any wand or staff and does not require a weapon for activation besides wielding an appropriate item capable of casting spells. Staves tend to be preferred due to inherent spell damage and increased cold damage modifiers, but wand builds focused on spell critical strike can also be effective.

## Leveling Use

Frostbolt is an effective leveling skill up to mid-game due to its solid balance of damage and crowd control. Early investments in cast speed and cold damage support gems provide smoother progression. Its piercing nature helps clear groups faster than many cold skills focused solely on single-target damage. 

Players should supplement Frostbolt with movement or defensive skills during leveling to remain mobile and safe. Transitioning from Frostbolt to other skills or adding painful damage layers like orb or totems may be needed by higher levels.

## Endgame Use

Endgame viability centers on stacking critical chance and cold penetration for boss fights and high-tier maps. Frostbolt can be a primary damage skill if well supported with pierce and critical strike boosts; otherwise, it serves well as a secondary utility skill paired with high single-target nukes or crowd control abilities.

Survivability upgrades and mana sustain become critical to maintain high uptime in the endgame. Players should monitor pierce interactions and elemental penetration as these often define Frostbolt’s ceiling. Testing personal gear and gem setups in endgame content is recommended for optimal tuning.

**Q: Can Frostbolt freeze enemies reliably?**
