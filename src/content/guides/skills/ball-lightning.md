## Overview

Ball Lightning offers a unique lightning spell experience focused on sustained, multi-hit damage with a projectile that lingers. Unlike auto-targeting lightning spells, its projectile positioning requires conscious placement, which can feel clunky early on. It excels when enemies remain stationary, allowing repeated shock applications and consistent damage output. However, its slower projectile speed often frustrates players, especially when clearing fast-moving packs, making it less optimal for pure speed clearing compared to skills like Arc or Spark.

## Skill Category

Ball Lightning is a Spell skill, falling squarely into the lightning caster archetype while providing a distinct projectile control dynamic compared to other lightning spells.

## Damage Type

The skill deals Lightning damage exclusively, benefiting from gear and passive nodes that boost lightning-specific damage or any spell-based lightning damage multipliers.

## Weapon Requirement

Ball Lightning requires the use of either a Wand or Staff to cast, so gearing choices should prioritize these weapon types to maximize skill effectiveness.

## Scaling Stats

This skill scales primarily with:

- Lightning Damage
- Spell Damage
- Area Damage (to improve the radius and potentially hit more enemies)
- Cast Speed (crucial to mitigate the slow projectile feel)
- Critical Strike Chance (to boost damage spikes and shock application)

Focusing on these stats ensures steady damage ramp-up and helps alleviate the skill’s inherent speed issues.

- **Slower Projectiles:** Although it reduces projectile speed, this support improves skill damage and control, which can ironically benefit positioning. The exact tradeoff on clear speed versus damage needs verification.

These supports collectively address Ball Lightning’s damage and usability quirks, focusing on improving sustained impact and projectile control.

## Recommended Builds

- **Lightning Sorceress:** Takes full advantage of the spell’s lightning scaling and shock synergy, paired with high cast speed for smoother gameplay.
- **Shock Ball Lightning Caster:** Emphasizes shock effects through critical strikes and controlled placement, maximizing single-target threat over time.

Both builds revolve around keeping Ball Lightning active on targets long enough for multiple hits and shocks, rather than burst clearing.

## Leveling Use

Early on, cast speed dominates the priority list because without it, the projectile and casting animation feel unwieldy and clunky. Newer players often struggle with projectile placement, mistakenly treating it like auto-targeting lightning spells, which leads to ineffective damage during leveling. Upgrading to relevant wand or staff gear and investing in cast speed nodes can resolve these issues noticeably. Damage spikes with each equipment upgrade commonly mark progression milestones, so expect some uneven feel early but smoother flow around mid-levels.

## Endgame Use

Ball Lightning shines in boss fights and scenarios where enemies linger within the projectile’s path, letting multiple hits and shock stacks accumulate. Its high sustained damage and reliable multi-hit mechanic make it a top choice for boss-focused lightning caster builds. However, it tends to fall behind in maps with fast, aggressive packs or modifiers that penalize projectile speed, requiring players to adjust positioning or support setups. For bosses, players commonly swap in even more controlled or single-target-oriented supports or add layers of utility to maintain uptime on their Ball Lightning.
