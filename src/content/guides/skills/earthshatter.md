## Overview

Earthshatter is a physical attack skill designed for players who prefer solid melee burst damage with crowd control potential. It excels in boss fights where precise timing of slams can punish open windows for heavy damage and stuns. The skill pairs well with armor-based defenses and straightforward weapon upgrades, making gearing fairly accessible. That said, its slow animation and commitment can be punishing in fast or chaotic encounters, and its clear speed usually lags behind more projectile-focused builds.

## Skill Category

Attack

Earthshatter is a melee attack skill requiring players to engage up close with the enemy. It is best thought of as a heavy-hitting slam that can disrupt enemy actions through stun effects while dealing physical damage.

## Damage Type

Physical

The skill deals purely physical damage, making physical damage scaling stats and defenses crucial to maximizing effectiveness and survivability.

## Weapon Requirement

Mace or two-handed melee weapon

To use Earthshatter, you must wield either a mace or a two-handed melee weapon. Weapon selection should focus on physical damage and stun chance to boost damage and control.

## Scaling Stats

- Physical damage  
- Melee damage  
- Stun buildup  
- Warcry effect  

The skill benefits strongly from boosts to physical and melee damage, increasing the raw damage output of each slam. Stun buildup scaling supports its secondary utility in locking down bosses or dangerous mobs. Warcry effect scaling is particularly useful, as it enhances overall damage and survivability synergy.

Each of these supports aims to optimize Earthshatter's physical damage output and crowd control synergy.

## Leveling Use

During leveling, Earthshatter requires consistent weapon upgrades any time damage begins to lag, as its attack speed and damage are heavily weapon-dependent. Many newer players neglect regular weapon swaps and experience steep damage drop-offs. Using warcries before tough rares and bosses can provide crucial boosts to damage and survivability.

Early investment in life and armor nodes is recommended because melee feels much safer with solid mitigation, especially since Earthshatter’s animation leaves you vulnerable during its wind-up and recovery frames. Players often find progression smoother by prioritizing survivability over raw offense until they can consistently land stuns and avoid damage.

## Endgame Use

Earthshatter is best suited for players who are comfortable reading boss attack patterns and timing their slams within safe punishment windows. It performs well against single-target enemies when properly supported but may require skill gem swaps depending on the fight.

Scaling physical mitigation alongside damage is essential in the endgame because the skill demands close contact and has high animation commitment. Players often swap out support gems to gear more single-target focused configurations during boss encounters to maximize damage efficiency.

Its slower clear speed means maps with heavy packs or those that reward fast area clear can feel frustrating. Adjustments to movement skills or additional clearspeed-focused setups may help alleviate this.
