## Overview  
Cold Snap is a channeled spell skill that unleashes a rapid sequence of icy explosions around the caster, dealing cold damage and applying chill effects to enemies in its radius. Renowned for its ability to clear packs quickly while maintaining control through frequent chilling, Cold Snap excels in both leveling and endgame scenarios where consistent area clear and crowd control are vital.

## Skill Category  
Cold Snap belongs to the Channeled Spell category. It requires the player to maintain the channel to continually trigger explosions, blending sustained damage output with crowd control potential through chill effects.

## Damage Type  
The primary damage type dealt by Cold Snap is Cold Damage. This makes it particularly effective against enemies susceptible to cold status ailments such as chill and freeze, improving survivability and damage dealt through various cold-related mechanics.

## Weapon Requirement  
Cold Snap can be used without a weapon requirement but benefits greatly from wands or sceptres that boost spell damage, cast speed, or cold damage. Using off-hands that enhance elemental damage or offer increased mana regeneration can also be advantageous to sustain channeling.

## Scaling Stats  
- **Spell Damage:** Directly increases the damage of each Cold Snap explosion.  
- **Cast Speed:** Higher cast speed shortens the interval between explosions, increasing overall damage and chill uptime.  
- **Cold Damage/Cold Penetration:** Amplifies damage and reduces enemy resistances for more consistent output.  
- **Mana Regeneration/Reservation:** Important for channel sustainability; a well-managed mana pool enables longer channel times.  
- **Increased Area of Effect or Area Damage:** Improves the number of targets affected per explosion, enhancing clear speed.

## Leveling Use  
Cold Snap is an excellent leveling skill due to its rapid AoE explosions and crowd control potential, which speeds clearing large monster packs. Coupled with movement skills like Flame Dash or Shield Charge, it allows safe positioning and continuous damage. Early game items that boost cast speed and cold damage will improve clear speed and survivability during level progression.

## Endgame Use  
In endgame content, Cold Snap thrives as a bossing and mapping skill due to reliable crowd control via chill and consistent damage output. Prioritize cold penetration, cast speed, and mana management to maintain uptime on bosses and high-HP targets. Defensive layers such as Energy Shield, evasion, or life regeneration ensure survivability during sustained channels. Experimentation with support gems and unique items that complement cold damage and channeling mechanics is recommended to tune performance.

## FAQ  
**Q: Does Cold Snap freeze enemies?**  
A: Cold Snap applies chill consistently, and enemies affected by enough cold damage and status can freeze; however, freeze chance depends heavily on gear and modifiers.

**Q: Can Cold Snap be used with spell totems?**  
A: Generally, Cold Snap is cast by the player and requires channeling, so it is not compatible with spell totems, as they cannot channel spells.

**Q: How important is cast speed for Cold Snap?**  
A: Cast speed is crucial because it controls how frequently explosions are triggered. Higher cast speed directly increases damage per second and chill uptime.

**Q: Does Cold Snap benefit from critical strikes?**  
A: Critical strikes can increase damage output, but Controlled Destruction support is often favored over critical chance supports due to more stable damage scaling.
