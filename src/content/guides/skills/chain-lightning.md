## Overview

Chain Lightning excels at clearing groups of enemies quickly thanks to its chaining mechanic and consistent shock uptime. It provides smooth mapping by bouncing between targets, making it feel fluid during large packs. However, its single-target damage is less impressive, which can become noticeable against bosses or strong rares. Players often find that mana consumption spikes at high cast speeds, so managing sustain is crucial. Early on, boosting cast speed and lightning damage helps progression, but as you scale, be ready to invest in mana sustain solutions to keep casting reliably.

## Skill Category

Chain Lightning is a spell, requiring a casting focus rather than melee or projectile mechanics. Its playstyle revolves around fast, repeated casts to maintain chain reactions and elemental status effects on enemies.

## Damage Type

This skill deals lightning damage, which synergizes well with shock effects and various lightning-specific damage bonuses. Its thematic shock application adds reliable crowd control and helps increase damage taken by enemies over time.

## Weapon Requirement

Chain Lightning requires a wand or staff equipped to cast, emphasizing the importance of one-handed or two-handed caster weapons that boost spell and lightning damage, alongside cast speed and critical stats.

## Scaling Stats

The skill scales primarily with sources of lightning damage and spell damage to boost total hit damage. Cast speed is vital for maintaining smooth chain procs and shock uptime. Critical strike chance also enhances burst potential, while increasing chain range improves target bounces, expanding effective pack clear.

## Best Supports

Supporting Chain Lightning with Lightning Penetration increases damage against enemies with high lightning resistances, enhancing effectiveness in later maps. Faster Casting is essential to maintain fluid chaining and maximize shock uptime. Controlled Destruction boosts damage output, though it reduces critical chance, so it’s best used when crit scaling is moderate. Added Lightning Damage enhances base damage and can increase shock potential, making each cast hit harder.

## Recommended Builds

The Lightning Sorceress build leverages Chain Lightning’s natural synergy with elemental and crit scaling, focusing on powerful spellcasting with strong map clear and shock uptime. Shock Caster builds emphasize maximizing shock effect duration and damage debuffs to enemies, pairing well with supports that enhance elemental status effects.

## Leveling Use

During leveling, prioritize increasing cast speed and lightning damage to keep clears efficient and comfortable. Players often hit a mana wall as cast speed rises, so managing mana regeneration or investment in sustain becomes necessary early. Common mistakes include focusing too heavily on crit or chain range too soon, which delays solid damage ramp-up. Stick to boosting damage and speed first for smooth progress.

## Endgame Use

Chain Lightning shines in dense map layouts with plentiful mobs, where its chaining mechanic can bounce between multiple targets efficiently. Its high chain value makes mapping feel quick and seamless. However, experienced players often pair it with separate single-target setups for bosses or unique enemies, as the skill’s single-target damage tends to lag behind dedicated burst spells. Being mindful of encounters with few enemies or map mods that reduce chaining can expose its limitations.

## FAQ

**Q: Does increasing chain range always improve performance?**  
A: Generally yes, as it allows the skill to hit more enemies per cast, but diminishing returns can occur depending on pack density. Prioritize this after securing cast speed and damage.

**Q: How critical is critical strike chance for Chain Lightning?**  
A: It provides strong burst potential, but controlled destruction support lowers crit chance, so a balanced crit setup is usually preferred over heavy crit investment early.

**Q: What are common mana sustain solutions?**  
A: Reserving or auras that boost mana regen, plus investment in gear with mana or mana regen, are typical solutions as cast speed ramps up.

**Q: Should I use Chain Lightning for boss fights?**  
A: Not usually by itself. Many players swap to a dedicated single-target skill or setup to handle bosses efficiently.
