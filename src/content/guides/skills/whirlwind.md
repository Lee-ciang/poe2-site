## Overview

Whirlwind is a melee attack skill that excels at fluid, mobile combat with strong area damage. It allows you to spin through packs of enemies, delivering repeated physical hits while staying on the move. This makes it ideal for players who want fast-paced gameplay and efficient clearing. However, its damage against bosses tends to lag behind without heavy scaling, and since it often places you close to enemies, solid defenses are essential for survival.

## Scaling Stats

Whirlwind predominantly scales with Physical Damage, Melee Damage, Attack Speed, Area Damage, and Movement Speed. 

Priority typically goes to boosting physical and melee damage to increase each hit’s power, alongside attack speed to ramp up total hits. Area damage improves clear potential by increasing damage over multiple enemies, while movement speed enhances the skill’s smoothness and responsiveness in combat.

## Best Supports

The ideal support gems maximize physical damage output, attack speed, and area coverage:

- **Brutality**: Greatly increases physical damage, amplifying each hit’s impact at the cost of elemental damage (irrelevant here). This is core to maximizing Whirlwind damage.
  
- **Melee Physical Damage**: Boosts the physical damage component and benefits scaling that applies specifically to melee attacks, increasing sustained damage output.

- **Faster Attacks**: Speeds up attack animations, crucial for maintaining continuous damage and movement without downtime.

- **Increased Area of Effect**: Expands the radius of the hits, improving pack clear significantly while you spin through groups.

These supports together enhance the fundamental strengths of Whirlwind, making each spin more devastating and fluid.

## Recommended Builds

- **Spin Warrior**: Focuses on combining heavy melee physical damage with attack speed and mobility to become a whirlwind of destruction across mobs.

- **Physical Cyclone Fighter**: A build variant centered around sustained physical damage and effective area coverage, using Whirlwind for rapid pack clearing and consistent boss damage requiring further investment.

## Leveling Use

When leveling, it’s important to prioritize weapon upgrades and attack speed. These significantly boost your damage early on and allow the skill to feel smooth and responsive. Movement speed is also critical for maintaining momentum and reducing awkward pauses while spinning.

New players often struggle by undervaluing weapon damage or attack speed, which leads to feeling weak against early packs. Additionally, investing some points into area of effect can help manage clusters of enemies more efficiently, reinforcing Whirlwind’s core advantage.

## Endgame Use

Whirlwind excels in melee builds that emphasize mobile clearing and sustained physical damage against groups. However, this mobility comes with tradeoffs during tougher boss fights.

The skill requires strong defensive layers—life, resistances, or recovery—to survive constant proximity to enemies. Single-target damage can feel insufficient without dedicated scaling, so most players swap or supplement supports and gear for bosses.

While great for mapping and fast clear, Whirlwind doesn’t shine in encounters demanding burst damage or long-range control. Effective endgame use hinges on balancing offense and defense with well-rounded passive trees and equipment choices.

## FAQ

**Q: Why does Whirlwind feel weak against bosses?**  
A: The skill’s repeated-hit nature depends heavily on physical scaling and attack speed. Without strong gear and proper single-target investment, damage falls off, making boss fights slow or frustrating.

**Q: Can I use Whirlwind without a melee weapon?**  
A: No, the skill requires a melee weapon to function.

**Q: Which support gems should I prioritize first?**  
A: Brutality and Melee Physical Damage typically provide the biggest initial power spikes, followed by Faster Attacks and Increased Area of Effect for smoother spins and better pack clear.

**Q: How important is movement speed for this skill?**  
A: Very important for smooth gameplay. Increased movement speed helps maintain momentum, avoid awkward stops, and improves overall skill feel.
