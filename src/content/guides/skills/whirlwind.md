## Overview

Whirlwind is a melee attack skill for players who want constant movement, repeated hits, and strong pack-clearing momentum. It turns positioning into offense: instead of stopping for every swing, you move through enemies while dealing damage around you. That makes it appealing for campaign clearing, dense maps, and melee builds that want to stay active rather than trade one heavy hit at a time.

The skill's identity is different from ranged guides such as [Lightning Arrow](/guides/skills/lightning-arrow) or caster guides such as [Spark](/guides/skills/spark). Whirlwind asks you to be close to danger. In exchange, it gives smoother movement, repeated physical damage, and the ability to clear while repositioning. The better your defenses and weapon upgrades are, the more confidently you can use the skill's mobility.

It is a practical hub for melee players deciding whether to build around speed, area damage, or heavier attack patterns.

## Mechanics

Whirlwind scales through weapon damage, physical damage, melee damage, attack speed, area damage, movement quality, and defensive uptime. Because it is an attack skill, weapon upgrades are especially important. A weak weapon makes every support gem feel worse, while a strong weapon immediately improves both clear and boss pressure.

The repeated-hit nature of Whirlwind means attack speed can be valuable, but only when the character remains safe and resource sustain keeps up. Faster hits do not help if you are forced to stop constantly, run out of resources, or die inside enemy attacks. Area investment improves clear because more enemies are clipped while you move through packs. Physical and melee scaling improve the value of each hit.

Positioning is the real mechanic that separates a good Whirlwind build from a frustrating one. You want to pass through the edges of danger, not park inside the most dangerous ground effects. Against bosses such as [Executioner](/bosses/executioner) or [Endgame Titan](/bosses/endgame-titan), Whirlwind is strongest when used during safe movement windows rather than as an excuse to ignore mechanics.

## Best Support Gems

Whirlwind usually wants supports that improve physical damage, melee damage, attack speed, and area coverage. Brutality-style physical scaling can be strong when the build is focused on physical damage. Melee Physical Damage improves the core hit. Faster Attacks makes the skill smoother and increases hit frequency. Increased Area of Effect can make mapping feel much better by widening the zone you threaten.

For bosses, consider whether area coverage is still needed. If clear is already comfortable, more single-target damage or stronger physical scaling may be better than additional coverage. If the skill feels clunky, attack speed and movement-related comfort can be worth more than a small damage increase.

Support choices should match the build's defenses. A fragile character should not use Whirlwind as if it were invulnerable. Damage supports matter, but so do the defensive layers that let you stay close enough to deal damage.

## Strengths

Whirlwind's biggest strength is mobile clearing. It lets melee characters keep momentum through packs and reduces the stop-start feeling that some attack skills have. This is especially valuable in zones where monsters approach from many angles.

It also rewards weapon upgrades clearly. When you improve the weapon, the skill immediately feels stronger. This makes progression easy to understand for melee players. The same is true for area and attack speed upgrades: the gameplay feedback is direct.

## Weaknesses

Whirlwind's main weakness is proximity. You deal damage by being near enemies, so poor defenses are punished quickly. If resistances, recovery, life, armor, evasion, or other defensive layers are behind the content, the skill can feel reckless.

Single-target damage can also lag without investment. Whirlwind clears packs through repeated area hits, but bosses require sustained uptime and enough damage per second to justify staying close. If you cannot safely maintain contact, damage falls sharply.

The skill can also encourage bad habits. Spinning through every danger zone may work in easy content but fails when bosses punish greed. Whirlwind is mobile, not immune.

## Leveling

When leveling Whirlwind, upgrade weapons as often as possible. Weapon damage is the foundation. Add physical damage, attack speed, and area coverage after the weapon is keeping pace. If the skill feels weak early, the first question should be whether the weapon is outdated.

Use Whirlwind to clear packs while staying in motion, but do not overcommit to every rare or boss. If a target is dangerous, circle the edge of its range, attack during safer windows, and leave space to dodge. Defensive passives and gear are not optional for melee leveling.

Whirlwind can share a progression path with other melee tools. [Earthshatter](/skills/earthshatter) offers heavier impact, [Ice Strike](/skills/ice-strike) adds elemental control, and [Tempest Bell](/skills/tempest-bell) can support combo-oriented monk setups. The best leveling route depends on whether you want pure physical momentum or a hybrid melee plan.

## Endgame

In endgame, Whirlwind is strongest as a mapping and mobility-focused melee skill. It can clear dense packs quickly when area coverage and weapon damage are strong. For bosses, it needs defenses, recovery, and disciplined timing. You should know when to enter, when to leave, and when a boss animation makes sustained contact unsafe.

Against [Executioner](/bosses/executioner), [Endgame Titan](/bosses/endgame-titan), and [King in the Mists](/bosses/king-in-the-mists), treat Whirlwind as a tool for controlled uptime. Do not spin through major telegraphed attacks. Use the skill to reposition around safe angles and punish recovery windows.

Endgame support choices should be content-specific. Mapping can favor area and speed. Bossing can favor damage and survivability. A balanced setup often performs better than one that only maximizes clear speed.

## Common Mistakes

The biggest mistake is ignoring weapon upgrades. Whirlwind is an attack skill, and an outdated weapon will make the entire setup feel bad. Another common mistake is stacking attack speed without solving defenses or resource sustain.

Players also overuse the skill inside boss danger zones. Whirlwind moves, but it does not make the character invincible. Good melee play uses the movement to avoid damage, not to absorb it.

## FAQ

### Is Whirlwind good for leveling?

Yes, if you keep weapons upgraded and invest in enough defenses. It is smooth for clearing packs but can punish careless melee positioning.

### What stats should Whirlwind prioritize?

Weapon damage, physical damage, melee damage, attack speed, area damage, movement comfort, and defensive layers are the core priorities.

### Why does Whirlwind feel weak against bosses?

Bosses require sustained safe uptime. If your weapon, single-target scaling, or defenses are weak, you cannot stay close long enough to deal strong damage.

### Does Whirlwind need a specific build?

It works best in melee builds that support physical damage, attack speed, area damage, and survivability. [Earthshatter Warrior](/builds/earthshatter-warrior) is a natural comparison point.

### Is Whirlwind safer than other melee skills?

It can be safer because it keeps you moving, but it still requires close range. Movement helps only when you use it to avoid dangerous attacks.

### Which skills pair well with Whirlwind?

[Earthshatter](/skills/earthshatter), [Ice Strike](/skills/ice-strike), [Tempest Bell](/skills/tempest-bell), and [Poison Arrow](/guides/skills/poison-arrow) can support different melee or hybrid progression paths.
