## Overview

Lightning Arrow excels at clearing packs quickly when paired with chain or projectile supports. Its innate ability to apply shock keeps follow-up damage consistent and impactful. Early on, the skill feels strong thanks to frequent bow upgrades that boost elemental damage. However, it relies heavily on your weapon and struggles against single targets without support swaps or additional skills. Managing mana can become tricky as attack speed ramps up, often requiring careful resource planning or passive investment.

## Skill Category

Lightning Arrow is a primary ranged attack skill, perfect for players favoring bow builds with fast-paced, lightning-based projectile damage.

## Damage Type

This skill deals lightning damage exclusively, meaning elemental penetration and shock scaling are key to maximizing output.

## Weapon Requirement

You must use a bow to activate Lightning Arrow, so consistent weapon upgrades are critical to progression. The skill’s damage scales prominently with your bow’s elemental damage stats.

## Scaling Stats

Focus on boosting weapon elemental damage, attack speed, lightning damage, and critical strike chance. These amplify both your clear speed and single-target potential when combined with the right supports.

## Best Supports

- **Chain**: Significantly enhances pack clear by allowing attacks to bounce between enemies.
- **Elemental Damage**: Increases overall elemental damage, boosting the skill’s core lightning output.
- **Lightning Penetration**: Helps bypass enemy resistances, essential for harder content.
- **Faster Attacks**: Raises attack rate, accelerating damage and shock application, but be mindful of increased mana costs.

Choosing supports that complement each other ensures smoother clears and stronger shocks, critical for keeping damage high.

## Recommended Builds

- **Lightning Ranger Build**: Capitalizes on attack speed and elemental scaling for rapid clear and decent boss damage.
- **Grenade Mercenary Build**: Combines Lightning Arrow’s chain clearing with grenade mechanics for versatile elemental damage.

## Leveling Use

Upgrade bows frequently and prioritize sources of added lightning damage to keep damage spikes consistent. Incorporate chain or pierce supports early for smoother campaign progression, reducing downtime and frustration. Defensive nodes may be necessary if you find positioning with a bow too risky, especially against ranged or gap-closing enemies. A common leveling oversight is underinvesting in defensive options, which leads to preventable deaths.

## Endgame Use

Lightning Arrow shines in mapping scenarios focused on clearing large packs swiftly. For bosses or tougher single targets, incorporate dedicated single-target supports or secondary skills to cover its relative weakness. Once your defenses are stable, prioritize scaling shock effectiveness and critical multipliers to push damage further. Avoid maps with harsh elemental ailment penalties, as they can drastically undermine your damage output.

## FAQ

**Q: Why does my single-target damage feel weak?**  
A: Lightning Arrow’s single-target potential hinges on your supports and gear. Swap in single-target focused gems or use a secondary skill for bosses.

**Q: How often should I upgrade my bow?**  
A: As often as possible, especially when leveling. Missing upgrades significantly hurts your damage scaling.

**Q: Is mana sustain an issue?**  
A: Yes, particularly at high attack speeds. Manage attack speed gems and consider mana-regeneration passives or items.

**Q: Can I run Lightning Arrow without chain or pierce?**  
A: Technically yes, but clears will feel slower and less fluid. Chain or pierce is strongly recommended for smoother gameplay.
