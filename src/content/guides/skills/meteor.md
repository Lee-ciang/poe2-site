## Overview

Meteor is a high-burst fire spell that excels at dealing massive area damage on impact. It is well-suited for players who prioritize burst damage and area control, especially against enemies that move slowly or remain stationary. While its damage is impressive, the delayed impact timing can make it feel clunky to use, and fast-moving targets often avoid the damage altogether, requiring some careful positioning and prediction.

## Skill Category

Meteor is a Spell skill, focusing on spellcasting rather than attack-based scaling.

## Damage Type

The damage dealt by Meteor is purely Fire-based, making elemental fire damage scaling and penetration key to maximizing its output.

## Weapon Requirement

This skill requires either a Wand or a Staff to cast, reinforcing its role as a caster-focused option rather than a melee or projectile skill.

## Scaling Stats

Meteor’s damage scales primarily from Fire Damage, Spell Damage, and Area Damage bonuses. Critical Strike Chance and Cast Speed are also important to improve its overall output and responsiveness. Prioritizing cast speed early can help alleviate some of the skill’s innate delay.

## Best Supports

- **Fire Penetration:** Essential for overcoming enemy fire resistances, boosting Meteor’s effective damage significantly.
- **Controlled Destruction:** Increases spell damage at the cost of critical strike chance. This tradeoff can be beneficial unless your build heavily depends on crit.
- **Increased Area of Effect:** Expands meteor’s impact zone, improving coverage and making it easier to hit multiple targets or control space.
- **Spell Echo:** Allows for faster consecutive casts, which helps counter Meteor’s slow projectile travel and cooldown rhythm.

## Recommended Builds

- **Fire Burst Sorceress:** Focused on quick, high-damage bursts with strong area coverage; leverages cast speed and fire scaling.
- **Crit Meteor Caster:** Builds emphasizing critical strikes alongside Meteor for sustained high damage; requires balancing crit chance and Controlled Destruction support.

## Leveling Use

Early leveling with Meteor can feel cumbersome due to the impact delay and mana consumption. Prioritize cast speed and mana sustain early on to smooth out the clunky animation and improve casting frequency. Meteor’s real strength emerges once you can invest in area and fire damage scaling, at which point it transitions from a niche leveling skill to a high-damage burst option. A common mistake is neglecting cast speed, which makes the skill feel slow and frustrating against roaming monsters.

## Endgame Use

Meteor remains a powerful burst tool for both boss fights and dense monster packs when enemies remain within its predicted impact zones. It shines in encounters where foes are relatively stationary or at least predictable, allowing you to land devastating area damage. However, experienced players often swap in faster or more flexible skills when facing very mobile bosses or enemies that avoid impact zones easily. Meteor’s performance can suffer in maps with heavy movement or tricky positioning, so a sensible build incorporates solutions for those situations.

## FAQ

**Q: Can Meteor hit moving targets reliably?**  
A: Meteor struggles against fast-moving enemies because of its delayed impact point. Positioning and predicting enemy movement are critical.

**Q: Should I prioritize crit or non-crit scaling?**  
A: Both approaches work, but crit builds typically require careful balancing of Controlled Destruction and crit chance. Non-crit builds rely more heavily on raw fire and spell damage.

**Q: Is cast speed absolutely necessary?**  
A: Yes. Without cast speed, Meteor feels sluggish and frustrating, especially when leveling.

**Q: Can I use weapons other than wands or staves?**  
A: No, Meteor requires a wand or staff to cast.
