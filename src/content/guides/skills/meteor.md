## Overview

Meteor is a fire spell built around delayed impact, heavy burst, and area control. It rewards prediction more than reflex. When you place the impact zone correctly, Meteor can punish dense packs and bosses that commit to long animations. When you cast without reading movement, the delayed hit can miss or land after the important window has passed.

This page matters because Meteor explains a different side of fire caster gameplay than [Fireball](/guides/skills/fireball) or [Flame Wall](/guides/skills/flame-wall). Fireball is direct, Flame Wall controls space, and Meteor offers burst with timing risk. Players deciding between fire skills need to understand that tradeoff before building around it.

Meteor also connects to important site clusters: [Infernal Witch](/builds/infernal-witch), [Ember Fusillade](/skills/ember-fusillade), [Fire Warden](/bosses/fire-warden), [Chimera Abomination](/bosses/chimera-abomination), and other caster pages. A strong Meteor guide gives readers a realistic view of where delayed fire damage shines and where it struggles.

## Mechanics

Meteor scales through fire damage, spell damage, area damage, cast speed, critical strike investment, ignite or ailment investment where supported, and fire penetration. Its most important mechanic is the delay before impact. That delay is both a weakness and a strength. It can miss mobile enemies, but it can also let you pre-place damage where enemies are about to stand.

The skill rewards encounter knowledge. Against bosses, cast Meteor when the target begins a committed animation or when you know where it will move next. Against packs, drop it where enemies are grouped or where they must pass through. The more predictable the enemy movement, the stronger Meteor becomes.

Meteor pairs well with faster or more consistent skills. [Fireball](/guides/skills/fireball) can handle direct casting, [Flame Wall](/guides/skills/flame-wall) can control space, and [Spark](/guides/skills/spark) or [Ice Spear](/guides/skills/ice-spear) offer useful comparisons for players exploring caster options outside fire.

## Best Support Gems

Fire Penetration is a priority for serious content because Meteor needs its heavy hits to matter against resistant enemies. Controlled Destruction or high spell damage supports can strengthen the impact. Increased Area of Effect improves pack coverage and makes the delayed strike more forgiving. Faster Casting improves the setup rhythm, though it does not remove the need for good placement.

Critical supports are strong only if the build invests enough into crit chance and multiplier. Ignite-focused support choices require a clear ailment plan. Avoid mixing too many damage identities without enough gear to support them.

For mapping, area and cast comfort may be more valuable. For bosses, penetration, hit damage, and timing matter most. Meteor should feel deliberate, not random.

## Strengths

Meteor's biggest strength is burst. A well-timed impact can remove dangerous packs or heavily punish bosses during stationary windows. That makes it satisfying for players who enjoy planning attacks around enemy behavior.

The skill also has strong area control. Even when enemies move, the threat of a large impact zone can shape how you play the arena. It pairs well with fire builds that already want enemies grouped or slowed.

Meteor adds depth to the fire cluster by showing that not every fire spell is direct. It gives readers a reason to compare [Fireball](/guides/skills/fireball), [Flame Wall](/guides/skills/flame-wall), [Ember Fusillade](/skills/ember-fusillade), and [Infernal Witch](/builds/infernal-witch) from a gameplay perspective, not just a damage-type perspective.

## Weaknesses

Meteor's main weakness is reliability. Fast enemies, mobile bosses, and chaotic arenas can reduce the number of clean hits. The skill can feel clunky if you use it like a normal instant projectile.

Mana and cast rhythm can also be challenging. Heavy supports may make each cast expensive, and missed Meteors feel more punishing than missed low-cost spells. This makes resource sustain and careful timing important.

Finally, Meteor can be unsafe if you tunnel on landing the perfect hit. Bosses will punish players who stand still too long. The skill is powerful, but it still requires movement discipline.

## Leveling

Meteor can level well when used with patience, but it may feel slower than more direct spells. Keep caster weapons upgraded, invest in fire and spell damage, and add cast speed once mana allows. Use a direct skill like [Fireball](/guides/skills/fireball) if you need faster cleanup between Meteor casts.

During the campaign, practice placing Meteor ahead of enemy movement. Cast on choke points, boss recovery windows, and grouped enemies. If you cast at where enemies were a moment ago, the skill will feel much weaker than it is.

Defensively, do not stand still waiting to admire the impact. Cast, move, and let the Meteor land while you stay safe.

## Endgame

In endgame, Meteor is best as a planned burst skill or a main fire spell for players who enjoy prediction. Mapping setups should improve area coverage and cast rhythm. Bossing setups should prioritize fire penetration, damage uptime, and knowledge of safe windows.

Against [Fire Warden](/bosses/fire-warden), [Chimera Abomination](/bosses/chimera-abomination), or [Count Geonor](/bosses/count-geonor), Meteor is strongest when you cast during animations that keep the boss in place. Avoid throwing Meteors into movement phases unless you have a reason to believe the boss will return to the impact zone.

The skill's endgame value comes from converting knowledge into damage. The better you know a fight, the better Meteor becomes.

Meteor also benefits from planning your secondary skill package. In maps, a faster spell can clean up enemies that survive outside the impact zone. In boss fights, a direct skill can keep damage moving while waiting for the next safe Meteor window. This does not weaken Meteor's identity; it supports it. The more your build covers the moments between impacts, the more freely you can use Meteor for the heavy hits it is designed to deliver.

This is especially important for players moving from direct spells. Meteor rewards patience, but it should not create dead time. A strong setup keeps pressure active between impacts and uses Meteor when the enemy is most likely to stay inside the blast.

## Common Mistakes

The biggest mistake is casting Meteor reactively at fast targets instead of predicting movement. Another is building only for maximum damage while ignoring cast comfort and survival.

Players also forget that missed Meteors waste more time than missed quick spells. If accuracy is poor, adjust positioning or use a supporting skill for mobile enemies.

## FAQ

### Is Meteor good for leveling?

It can be, but it is more timing-dependent than direct spells. Pairing it with [Fireball](/guides/skills/fireball) can make leveling smoother.

### What stats should Meteor prioritize?

Fire damage, spell damage, area damage, fire penetration, cast speed, and either critical or ignite scaling if the build supports it.

### Why does Meteor miss so often?

The impact is delayed. Cast where the enemy will be during the impact, not only where it is when you press the button.

### Is Meteor better for bosses or mapping?

It can do both. Mapping rewards area coverage, while bossing rewards timing and penetration. It is strongest against predictable targets.

### Does Meteor need Fireball?

No, but [Fireball](/guides/skills/fireball) can cover faster direct damage when Meteor's delay is inconvenient.

### Can Meteor work in endgame?

Yes. It needs strong fire scaling, safe casting habits, and encounter knowledge to land high-value impacts consistently.
