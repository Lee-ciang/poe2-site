## Overview

Arc is a lightning spell built around automatic chaining, fast pack clear, and frequent shock application. It is one of the most approachable lightning spells because it handles much of the targeting work for you. Cast into a group, and Arc moves through nearby enemies without requiring perfect projectile aim.

That simplicity makes Arc valuable for beginners, but the skill still has depth. Its clear strength depends on enemy density, cast speed, lightning scaling, and shock value. Its boss strength depends on penetration, damage investment, and whether the build has a plan for isolated targets. Players who understand that distinction will get much more from the skill than players who only judge it by early campaign clear.

Arc is a central lightning authority page because it connects directly to [Spark](/guides/skills/spark), [Chain Lightning](/guides/skills/chain-lightning), [Ball Lightning](/guides/skills/ball-lightning), [Lightning Arrow](/guides/skills/lightning-arrow), and [Lightning Ranger](/builds/lightning-ranger). It gives readers a clean comparison point for every other lightning skill cluster.

## Mechanics

Arc scales through lightning damage, spell damage, cast speed, shock effect, lightning penetration, and critical strikes if the build supports them. Its chain mechanic lets the spell jump between nearby enemies, which makes pack density extremely valuable. The more targets are available, the better Arc's natural behavior feels.

Because Arc targets automatically after the first cast, it reduces aiming friction. That does not mean positioning is irrelevant. Standing where enemies are grouped, casting at central targets, and maintaining range from danger all improve results. Against isolated bosses, the chain mechanic contributes less, so raw damage and penetration become more important.

Shock is one of Arc's major advantages. Frequent lightning hits can help maintain shock uptime, making follow-up damage stronger. This creates synergy with other lightning pages and builds, including [Spark](/guides/skills/spark), [Ball Lightning](/guides/skills/ball-lightning), and [Lightning Arrow](/skills/lightning-arrow).

## Best Support Gems

Lightning Penetration is a high-priority support because it helps Arc remain effective against resistant enemies. Faster Casting improves clear speed and shock uptime. Added Lightning Damage or spell damage supports can strengthen early and midgame performance. Critical supports are worth considering only when the build has enough crit investment.

Do not overinvest in clear if clear is already solved. Arc often clears packs well before it has enough boss damage. Once mapping feels smooth, support choices should shift toward penetration, stronger hits, and reliable uptime.

Mana sustain should also guide support choices. Faster casting is powerful, but a build that cannot afford its casts will lose damage in real encounters.

## Strengths

Arc's biggest strength is low-friction clearing. It quickly damages groups without requiring careful projectile paths. This makes it comfortable for campaign progression and efficient mapping.

The skill also provides strong visual and mechanical feedback. Players can see the chain behavior, understand when density helps, and feel the impact of cast speed upgrades. That makes Arc a good teaching skill for lightning mechanics.

Arc has excellent internal linking value. From this guide, readers can explore [Chain Lightning](/guides/skills/chain-lightning) for another chain-focused spell, [Spark](/guides/skills/spark) for projectile coverage, [Ball Lightning](/guides/skills/ball-lightning) for sustained area pressure, and [Lightning Arrow](/guides/skills/lightning-arrow) for a bow attack version of the lightning theme.

## Weaknesses

Arc's main weakness is isolated single-target damage. When there are no extra enemies to chain through, the skill needs strong damage scaling to keep up. This is why some players pair Arc with a boss-focused skill or adjust supports for difficult encounters.

The skill can also encourage passive play. Because it targets easily, players may stand still too long or ignore defensive layers. In endgame, that becomes a serious issue.

Another weakness is gear scaling. Arc can feel strong early, but if wand or staff upgrades fall behind, bosses reveal the problem quickly. Cast speed and penetration cannot fully compensate for weak base damage.

## Leveling

Arc is excellent for leveling because it clears packs with minimal aiming. Upgrade caster weapons frequently, take lightning and spell damage, and add cast speed when mana sustain can support it. If mana becomes unstable, slow down support intensity until recovery improves.

Aim at central enemies in packs to maximize chain value. For bosses, use safe casting windows and consider a secondary single-target tool if the fight drags. Do not let easy clear distract from defenses; life, resistances, and recovery still matter.

Arc also gives players a natural path into the wider lightning cluster. If you enjoy automatic chains, compare it with [Chain Lightning](/guides/skills/chain-lightning). If you want more screen coverage, try [Spark](/guides/skills/spark). If you want sustained overlap, test [Ball Lightning](/guides/skills/ball-lightning).

## Endgame

In endgame mapping, Arc shines in dense content. Cast speed, penetration, and shock scaling can make it feel fast and responsive. It is especially satisfying when packs are close enough for chains to continue naturally.

Bossing requires a different mindset. Against [Count Geonor](/bosses/count-geonor), [Executioner](/bosses/executioner), or [Endgame Titan](/bosses/endgame-titan), prioritize safe uptime and stronger single-target scaling. If adds are present, Arc can use them well. If the boss is isolated, the build must carry damage through gear and supports.

Arc remains one of the best pages for explaining lightning fundamentals because it shows both the power and limitation of chain-based damage.

Arc also helps players diagnose their lightning builds quickly. If pack clear is excellent but bosses feel slow, the issue is probably not the chain mechanic; it is usually single-target scaling, penetration, or uptime. If both clear and bossing feel weak, the weapon, spell damage, or cast speed is likely behind the content. This makes Arc a useful teaching page as well as a skill guide, because its strengths and weaknesses are easy to feel in normal play.

That diagnostic value makes Arc a strong upgrade target. It gives readers a simple path from first impressions to practical fixes, then points them toward nearby lightning pages when they want a different damage pattern.

It is also a reliable baseline for testing lightning gear because changes to cast speed, penetration, and shock effect are easy to feel during normal play.

## Common Mistakes

The most common mistake is assuming Arc's campaign clear means boss damage is solved. Another is ignoring mana sustain while stacking cast speed. Both mistakes reduce real damage when fights last longer.

Players also sometimes stand still because Arc aims easily. The skill is convenient, but endgame survival still requires movement and defenses.

## FAQ

### Is Arc good for beginners?

Yes. Arc is easy to aim, clears packs well, and teaches lightning scaling in a very readable way.

### What stats should Arc prioritize?

Lightning damage, spell damage, cast speed, lightning penetration, shock effect, and critical scaling if your gear supports it.

### Why does Arc feel weak against bosses?

Arc gains a lot from chaining through packs. Isolated bosses require stronger single-target scaling, penetration, and better damage uptime.

### Is Arc better than Chain Lightning?

They are similar, but [Arc](/guides/skills/arc) is usually more automatic while [Chain Lightning](/guides/skills/chain-lightning) emphasizes directed chaining.

### Can Arc be used in endgame?

Yes, especially for mapping. Endgame bossing requires a focused damage plan and reliable defenses.

### Which skills pair well with Arc?

[Spark](/guides/skills/spark), [Ball Lightning](/guides/skills/ball-lightning), [Chain Lightning](/guides/skills/chain-lightning), and [Ice Spear](/guides/skills/ice-spear) all cover different needs.
