## Overview  
Frostbolt offers dependable cold damage with strong crowd control, thanks to its chill and freeze effects. It’s a staple for ranged spellcasters who want to reliably clear packs while maintaining defensive control over the battlefield. However, early leveling can be rough on mana, and the projectile speed often feels sluggish until you invest in cast speed or other projectile-enhancing supports. Knowing when to upgrade your wand or staff to keep damage relevant is key to smoothing progression.

## Skill Category  
Spell — Frostbolt belongs firmly in the spellcasting category, relying on wand or staff synergy to maximize its cold damage.

## Damage Type  
Cold — All damage dealt is cold-based, allowing synergy with ailments like Chill and Freeze and benefiting heavily from cold damage modifiers and penetration.

## Weapon Requirement  
Wand or Staff — Using the appropriate weapon type matters here. You’ll want to upgrade wands or staves often to keep your spell damage competitive, especially in early to mid-level stages where damage spikes can stall progression.

## Scaling Stats  
Cold Damage, Spell Damage, Projectile Damage, Critical Strike Chance, Cast Speed — These stats boost Frostbolt's damage output and casting efficiency in different ways. Cold and spell damage are your core damage scalers, while critical strike chance and cast speed multiply overall damage and improve clear speed.

## Best Supports  
- **Cold Penetration**: Essential for bypassing enemy cold resistances, significantly increasing damage output against tougher foes.  
- **Hypothermia**: Amplifies damage against chilled or frozen enemies, making the innate chill and freeze effects more rewarding.  
- **Controlled Destruction**: Improves base spell damage without sacrificing critical chance, which synergizes well with crit builds.  
- **Faster Casting**: Addresses Frostbolt’s naturally slow projectile speed and casting animation, improving both clear speed and overall feel.

## Recommended Builds  
- **Cold Sorceress**: Focuses on cold damage boosts and reliable freeze effects to control crowds and bosses alike.  
- **Crit Frost Mage**: Leverages high critical strike chance and multiplier to maximize burst damage, paired with critical damage scaling supports.

## Leveling Use  
Early leveling often feels mana-starved and the projectile’s slower initial speed can make clearing inefficient. Prioritize gear and passives that boost cast speed and cold damage early on to overcome these issues. Frequent wand upgrades are critical because relying on one weapon too long results in significant damage stagnation. Newer players sometimes mistakenly neglect cast speed, making pack clearing tedious.

## Endgame Use  
Frostbolt remains a go-to for cold-based caster builds emphasizing freeze and critical strike scaling. It excels against dense packs with its crowd control and benefits greatly from cold penetration and faster casting setups. However, bosses or heavily mobile targets often require swapping supports or using different skills to compensate for its slower projectile travel time. Defensive layers should not be overlooked, as relying on freeze alone is risky in high-tier maps.

## FAQ  
**Q: Why does Frostbolt feel slow and clunky at times?**  
A: Its base projectile speed is on the slower side. Prioritizing cast speed, either from gear or supports like Faster Casting, makes a noticeable difference.

**Q: Is Frostbolt good for solo play?**  
A: Yes, especially in builds that lean into freeze mechanics for survivability, but additional defensive options are recommended for tougher bosses.

**Q: How important is wand/staff upgrading?**  
A: Very. Without frequent upgrades, your spell damage—and by extension your clear speed and boss potential—can plateau early.

**Q: Can Frostbolt handle bosses well?**  
A: With critical scaling and cold penetration, yes. But slower projectiles can make dodging or burst damage windows tricky without the right gear and supports.
