## Overview  
Fireball is a reliable ranged spell favored for its strong ignite scaling and explosive pack-clear potential. It hits hard against groups, providing satisfying AoE bursts while applying consistent ignite damage over time. However, progression can feel uneven—damage heavily leans on cast speed and projectile bonuses, and boss hits often demand significant investment to stay relevant. Players frequently notice a damage dip if they neglect early cast speed or struggle without proper weapon upgrades.

## Skill Category  
Fireball is a spell skill, primarily benefiting from enhancements that boost elemental and projectile spellcasting. Its design favors wand or staff users who can leverage high casting speed and elemental damage stats.

## Damage Type  
This skill deals fire damage, relying on ignite effects to amplify damage over time and synergizing with fire-resistance reduction mechanics. Its explosions contribute substantial burst damage to fights with multiple foes.

## Weapon Requirement  
Wand or staff usage is mandatory for Fireball, as these weapons maximize spellcasting stats like cast speed and elemental damage. Frequent weapon upgrades during leveling are crucial; staying on outdated wands or staves can lead to noticeable damage plateaus.

## Scaling Stats  
Fireball’s damage relies heavily on scaling with Fire Damage, Spell Damage, Projectile Damage, Cast Speed, and Critical Strike Chance. Cast speed is particularly important early on to maintain consistent clear speed and ignite application. Critical strike chances contribute more in specialized crit builds, while projectile bonuses increase the explosion radius and impact.

## Best Supports  
- **Fire Penetration** – Essential for bypassing enemy fire resistances, significantly boosting damage, especially against tougher mobs and bosses.  
- **Controlled Destruction** – Increases spell damage at the cost of crit chance, beneficial for non-crit builds focusing on raw elemental power.  
- **Faster Casting** – Clears packs more quickly, allowing faster ignite stacks and smoother gameplay. Lack of cast speed frequently makes Fireball feel sluggish.  
- **Ignite Proliferation** – Greatly enhances the skill’s ignite-based damage by spreading ignites between enemies, turning Fireball into a potent AoE burn spreader.

## Recommended Builds  
- **Ignite Sorceress**: Capitalizes on ignite stacking and proliferation, with heavy fire damage and cast speed investment to make Fireball’s burns lethal over time.  
- **Crit Fire Mage**: Focuses on critical strikes and projectile damage to maximize burst explosions and single-target damage, offering solid boss uptime when well-geared.

## Leveling Use  
Prioritize cast speed and fire damage stats early to keep Fireball responsive and effective in clearing groups. A common mistake is neglecting weapon upgrades; low-level wands/staves quickly become a bottleneck, causing damage to drop off noticeably. Upgrading weapons regularly mitigates this and smooths progression. Expect a damage lull when transitioning from early leveling setups to higher-end gear, especially before critical strike or penetration nodes come online.

## Endgame Use  
Fireball remains a popular skill for ignite-focused caster builds, excelling in map clear through ignite proliferation and explosions. It performs best when fully invested in fire penetration and critical strike scaling to handle tougher bosses. However, without such investment, boss damage can feel underwhelming. Experienced players often swap to enhanced single-target alternatives or support gems for boss fights to compensate.

## FAQ  
**Q: Why does Fireball feel weak against bosses?**  
A: Boss damage is highly dependent on crit chance and fire penetration investment. Without these, single-target output falls short compared to packs.

**Q: Is Faster Casting really necessary?**  
A: Yes, without sufficient cast speed, Fireball’s clear rate and ignite application slow, making early and mid-game frustrating.

**Q: Can I use Fireball without wands or staves?**  
A: No, the skill requires a wand or staff to cast, and improper weapons drastically reduce damage and cast speed.

**Q: What should I focus on upgrading first?**  
A: Prioritize cast speed and fire damage on gear, and upgrade your wand or staff regularly to avoid damage plateaus during leveling.
