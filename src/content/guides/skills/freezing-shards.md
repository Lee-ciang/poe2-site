## Overview  

Freezing Shards is a cold projectile spell best known for its rapid hit rate, enabling quick freeze stack buildup on enemies. It excels at clearing dense packs swiftly thanks to its frequent hits, making it a solid choice for players who want to control crowds while applying strong crowd control. However, the skill’s relatively short range compared to other cold spells requires attentive positioning, especially against aggressive or fast enemies. Many players find the clear speed satisfying once cast speed thresholds are met, though its safety window can feel narrow when soloing or in tougher encounters.  

## Skill Category  

Freezing Shards belongs to the Spell category, focusing on casting cold projectiles rather than melee or minion attacks.  

## Damage Type  

The skill deals Cold damage, which synergizes well with ailments like freeze and chills, adding utility beyond raw damage.  

## Weapon Requirement  

Freezing Shards requires either a Wand or Staff to cast, directing gearing priorities to these weapon types for optimal scaling.  

## Scaling Stats  

This skill scales primarily with Cold Damage, Spell Damage, Projectile Damage, Cast Speed, and Critical Strike Chance. Prioritizing these stats will increase damage per hit and overall frequency of casts, enhancing both damage output and crowd control application.  

## Best Supports  

- **Cold Penetration:** Essential for cutting through enemy cold resistance, this support significantly improves damage against tougher targets.  
- **Faster Casting:** Because Freezing Shards relies heavily on hit frequency, Faster Casting dramatically improves both damage output and freeze uptime, smoothing out the casting feel.  
- **Greater Multiple Projectiles:** Increasing the number of projectiles boosts clear speed and crowd control potential, but watch for projectile spread diluting single-target damage (this interaction needs verification under patch changes).  
- **Hypothermia:** Enhances damage against chilled enemies, reinforcing the skill’s freeze-based control theme.  

These supports balance damage and utility, leveraging the skill’s fast casting and freezing capabilities.  

## Recommended Builds  

- **Freeze Sorceress:** Focuses on freeze ailments and cold damage, using Freezing Shards for reliable chill and crowd control.  
- **Cold Projectile Caster:** Prioritizes projectile and spell damage synergies with cold penetration and crit, maximizing damage and clear speed.  

Both builds rely heavily on hit frequency and freeze mechanics to capitalize on the skill’s strengths.  

## Leveling Use  

Freezing Shards benefits greatly from early investment in cast speed; leveling without prioritizing cast speed can feel sluggish and unsatisfying. Positioning is key—players should be close enough to maximize projectile hits for freeze buildup, but avoid overextending into dangerous enemy packs too early. A common mistake is trying to kite enemies too far, which drops hit frequency and slows progress. Early wand or staff upgrades that increase spell or cold damage are necessary to maintain damage pacing through normal and cruel difficulties.  

## Endgame Use  

In endgame content, Freezing Shards thrives in freeze-focused cold builds that can maintain rapid hit frequency for maximum crowd control uptime. However, its shorter effective range means players must stay aware of positioning, especially in boss fights or open map layouts where enemies can close gaps quickly. Many experienced players supplement their toolkit with mobility or defensive layers to mitigate this risk. The skill's clear speed is strong in dense maps but limited in larger, spread-out encounters or against enemies with high cold resistance unless well-optimized.  

## FAQ  

**Q: Why does Freezing Shards feel underwhelming in some maps?**  
A: Its skill range is shorter compared to other cold spells, making it struggle with open layouts or against ranged enemies that maintain distance. Positioning closer is necessary but riskier.  

**Q: Can I use Freezing Shards without wand or staff?**  
A: No, the skill requires a wand or staff to function, so your weapon choice directly impacts both damage and usability.  

**Q: Should I prioritize cast speed or cold damage first?**  
A: Cast speed upgrades early on greatly improve skill feel and clear speed; cold damage scaling becomes more impactful as you approach endgame.  

**Q: Is it worth adding Greater Multiple Projectiles?**  
A: Typically yes for clear speed, but it may slightly reduce single-target damage due to spread—this interaction should be tested for your specific build and patch version.
