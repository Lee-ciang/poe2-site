## Overview

Lightning Ranger is a fast ranged bow build focused on lightning damage, shock uptime, and smooth map clear. It is a good fit for players who want mobility and fast pack clearing without committing to melee positioning.

## Strategy / Usage

Use Lightning Arrow as the primary clear skill and keep moving between attacks. Upgrade your bow often, prioritize movement speed, and add defensive layers before pushing harder bosses or dangerous map modifiers.

## Strengths

- Excellent clear speed with lightning projectiles.
- Strong mobility for learning boss mechanics.
- Clear gear upgrade path through bow damage, attack speed, and lightning scaling.

## Weaknesses

- Can feel fragile before life, evasion, and resistances are stable.
- Single-target damage may need support swaps.
- Mana sustain can become a problem at high attack speed.

## FAQ

### Is Lightning Ranger beginner friendly?

Yes, as long as players keep their bow and defenses updated. The build is easier to pilot than many melee setups, but poor positioning can still be punished.

### What should I upgrade first?

Upgrade your bow first if damage feels low. Upgrade life, evasion, and resistances first if deaths are the main blocker.
